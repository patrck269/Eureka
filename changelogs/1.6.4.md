# 1.6.4

- Immersive Aircraft planes landed on a Eureka/VS ship no longer backflip or clip through the deck. Landing pose and contact are corrected in ship space (optional mixin, skipped if IA is absent).
- Stabilize no longer emits NaN torque when a ship is inverted (zero cross-product axis).
- Balloon lift no longer divides by zero when `maxBalloonsPerEngine` is enabled with zero balloons.
